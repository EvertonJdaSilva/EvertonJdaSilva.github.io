## Optimality conditions for irregular single-objective and multiobjective problems
Federal University of Paraná (January 2018 – December 2018)


This project involves the study of irregular optimization problems with one and multiple objectives. It aims to address them using the p-regularity theory introduced by Tretyakov and Marsden. We also seek to derive sufficient optimality conditions via this theory.
